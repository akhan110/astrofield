# AstroField UI Design Spec

## Product goal
Offline-first field companion for astrophotographers who may have GPS but no internet connection.

## Visual language
- Near-black astronomical background
- Cool blue/violet primary accents
- Teal for strong observing conditions / success
- Amber for moon, twilight and caution states
- Rounded cards with subtle borders rather than heavy shadows
- Large numbers for time, altitude and imaging scores

## Navigation
Bottom navigation:
- Home
- Sky
- Tonight
- Saved
- More

Secondary screens open with normal push navigation.

## Core UI concepts
- Astronomy Score: always available from on-device calculations
- Conditions Score: based on cached weather and clearly shows freshness
- Offline pack: explicit readiness before remote trips
- Every object detail exposes altitude, azimuth, rise/transit/set, magnitude and best imaging window
- Equipment profile enables future personalized ranking and FOV framing
